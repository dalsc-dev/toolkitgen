Key Instagram Metrics Explained

Several metrics matter for Instagram success:

  • Reach - How many unique accounts saw your content
  • Impressions - Total times your content was displayed
  • Engagement Rate - Interactions divided by reach or followers
  • Follower Growth - Net new followers over time
  • Saves - Times people saved your content

FAQ: Instagram Insights

1. How often should I check insights?

Check weekly for trends, daily for specific post performance.

2. What is a good engagement rate?

1-3% is average; 5%+ is excellent.

3. Why do my impressions exceed reach?

People see posts multiple times (impressions) from unique viewers (reach).

4. What's the best time to post?

Check your specific follower activity hours in insights.

5. How do I increase reach?

Create engaging content and use relevant hashtags.

6. Why did I lose followers?

Some unfollowing is normal. Review content changes.

7. What saves metric means?

High saves indicate valuable content people want to revisit.

8. Should I delete low-performing posts?

No, they can still drive reach. Just post better content.

9. How many hashtags to use?

3-5 relevant hashtags work better than max.

10. Are Reels necessary?

Yes, Reels significantly increase reach.

11. What's a good follower growth rate?

1-3% monthly is healthy for growing accounts.

12. Does posting frequency matter?

Consistency matters more than frequency.

13. Is it worth buying followers?

No - they don't engage and hurt algorithmic reach.

14. Why is engagement so low?

Review content quality, timing, and hashtag relevance.

15. What's the follower ratio?

Following-to-follower ratio has minimal algorithmic impact.

16. When do I get insights access?

Switch to professional account for insights.

17. How do I read Story insights?

Check replies, taps forward/back, and completion.

18. Should I reply to all comments?

Yes, engagement is key to algorithmic success.

19. Does carousel format work?

Yes, carousels often get higher engagement.

20. What's reach vs. impressions?

Reach = unique viewers; impressions = total views.
